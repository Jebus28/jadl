Format

1.The Jessica Alba Dynasty League (JADL) will use Sleeper’s fully managed format with weekly lineups, waivers, trades, head-to-head and playoffs.

2.The format is a 10-team year-round fantasy league, with an initial start-up draft of 25 rounds.

3.Each owner is required to draft a starting lineup of 1-QB, 2-RB, 2-WR, 1-TE, 2-FLEX, 1, SUPERFLEX, 1-K, 1-D/ST. If you do not draft a complete starting roster, the Commissioner may add a player at their discretion after the draft to ensure a valid roster.

4.During the offseason, each JADL team will protect 26 players (including at least 1 Kicker and 1 Defense/ST) and release up to 8 players.

5.Each off-season, following the NFL draft, there will be a 5 round rookie draft

6.Each off-season, two weeks prior to season start, there will be a Free Agency auction in which any player (or defense) not currently owned may be obtained by the highest bidder.

7.Prior to the start of the season, each owner must cut their roster down to 26 players.

Rules and League Structure

8.The League Season will begin the Friday PRIOR to Thursday’s Opening Game in NFL Week 1 and conclude after NFL Week 17. The JADL off-season will be defined as the remainder of the year other than Weeks 1 through 17. All references to “points”, “total points” or “points total” imply starting lineup points.

9.JADLs will each be comprised of 10 teams/participants/managers/owners and the regular season will run from Week 1 through Week 17.

10.In each of the subsequent years, there will be a Roster cut-down deadline after the conclusion of each NFL Season. All rosters must be cut-down from a maximum of 33 players to 26 players (not including Taxi or IR) by 11:59 pm on August 31st of each year. Failure to do so will result in the league commissioner cutting players for the team in question.

11.The Maiden (start-up) Draft will be held online, on a predetermined date and time during the off-season. A random drawing will be used to determine draft order at an online meeting as soon as possible after all ten player have registered their teams. It will be supplied via the Whatsapp group shortly thereafter.

12.The Maiden draft will be a live draft with a 120 second timer for each pick.

13.Owners are encouraged to draft as quickly as they can and should use their queue, one pick auto, rankings and other features to avoid issues during the draft.

14.League structure will be as follows:

15.Each team will play in 14 weekly regular season Head to Head match-ups in Weeks 1-14. Teams are divided into two 5-team conferences (divisions for the purposes of Sleeper).The 3 teams from each conference (subject to ‘Rule 3’ below) at the end of the regular season will play in League Playoffs in NFL Weeks 15, 16 & 17 to determine the Jessica Alba Dynasty League Champion. The top teams of each conference at the end of the regular season will be awarded a Regular Season prize. The top point scoring team will be awarded a Regular Season Prize. The top week scoring team will also be awarded a Regular Season Prize. Both regular season Conference winners will receive a Bye in the Week 15 Playoff Round.

Minimum Roster requirements

16.Each team’s roster must have a minimum of 1 QB, 2 RBs, 2 WRs, 1 TE, 1 K and 1 D/ST at all times during the JADL offseason. During the JADL Season, a 26-player roster is required with a minimum of one player at each required starting position: QB, RB, WR, TE, K, D/ST.

17.All dynasty owners must draft a complete starting lineup including a kicker and a defense in the Maiden startup draft. Failure to do so may result in the Commissioner cutting players as needed and adding a kicker and/or defense.

18.During the JADL off-season, rosters will be allowed to exceed, or be diminished by the yearly requirement of 26 players through trades, rookie draft acquisitions and free agent auction ONLY. Any team whose roster exceeds the yearly player limit will be required to release the extra players prior to the start of the JADL Season. The Dynasty roster cut down date is the 1st September prior to the day of the first game of the NFL season. All teams must be down to a maximum of 26 active players at that time. IR’ed and Taxi players do not count as part of the 26.

19.When the JADL Season begins, any team below the yearly player roster is permitted to make blind bid waiver wire acquisitions to meet the in-season roster requirements.

20.The starting lineup for JADL teams is 1 QB, 2 RB, 2WR, 1 TE, 2 FLEX (RB, WR, or TE), 1 SUPERFLEX (QB, RB, WR or TE), 1 K, 1 DEF/ST.

21.It is the responsibility of every owner to submit a competitive lineup every week of the JADL season. Tanking is expressly and strictly prohibited. The Rookie Draft system has been designed to avoid any advantage to finishing bottom of the league. The last place team will not by default receive the No.1 pick in the following Rookie Draft.

22.The Commissioner may (but is not required to) alter a lineup for any team where a starting lineup is submitted with players who are inactive as a result of being on a Bye Week, on suspension, or on their NFL team’s I.R. list and the team be subject to Sanction (see below). Lineups may also be changed if a very low-ranked player is started over a consensus top player, or for whatever reason if the Commissioner sees that the original lineup lessens the competitiveness of the team in question. Tanking in the form of not doing waivers, not moving Injured Reserve players to IR, and picking up low-quality players to minimize one’s score is also prohibited. The Commissioner has the final discretion on all lineup changes.

Disputes and Appeals

23.All disputes and appeals must be submitted to the league commissioner. The league commissioner’s decisions are considered final. The Commissioner reserve the right, at their discretion, to call for a vote by all league members to help decide certain issues.

Trading Rules

24.Trading is permitted. All trades are expected to be executed in good faith and with the goal of improving one’s roster over the short or long-term. Unethical trading or roster dumping are not permitted and can, in extreme circumstances, lead to trade reversal, sanctions, and banishment from the JADL without the return of deposit.

25.Trades are free of charge. A trade becomes official once both teams have accepted the terms of the trade.

26.Trades occurring during the JADL Season may involve unequal amounts of players and/or draft picks, but each team must adjust their roster as needed to stay at 26 players or less upon consummation of the trade. In-season trades may include future draft picks for the next three drafts ONLY. Players that already have played for the week cannot be traded after their game until the following week. The trade deadline during the JADL Season is 23:00 GMT on the Thursday of Week 16 of the NFL season. There is no trading between this deadline and the end of the NFL regular season.

27.Trades occurring during the JADL Off-season may involve an unequal number of players exchanged by both teams. Off-season trades may include future draft picks for the next three drafts ONLY. There is no trade deadline during the JADL Off-season. Trades can be made immediately after the completion of Week 17.

28.A trade can be protested by any team for consideration by the Commissioner. Only trades where there are genuine errors agreed by both parties in the trade before any points have been scored are likely to be overturned. The league is expected to be played in a friendly and competitive manner on the basis of mutual respect and trust. The Commissioner will only consider intervening in a trade in highly exceptional or extreme circumstances.

Injury Reserve Transactions

29.During the Season, a team may place a player on IR if they appear on their NFL teams Injured Reserve or Physically Unable to Practice Lists. The team may then replace the IR player and pick up a player via the blind bid waiver wire process. IR moves are free of charge. Any free agent who is listed on IR by an NFL team and is then drafted or acquired via the waiver wire is eligible for the IR list. A team may have a maximum of 5 players on the IR list at any one time. The official site for determining a player’s IR status will be Sleeper. Players on the IR list are eligible to be traded to another team. If an NFL team activates a player on IR to their active roster, owners must activate or cut that player within the next 3 days during the JADL season.

IR Qualification

30.Any player on NFL Injured Reserve, NFL Injured Reserve-Eligible to Return, NFL-PUP list, NFL-NFI (non-football injury) list can be placed on Injured Reserve. A player that is Suspended or Out for the week cannot be placed on Injured Reserve.

Yearly Rookie Draft

31.In each subsequent year after the Maiden draft, an off-season draft for rookies called the Rookie draft will be held. This will be after the second Friday after of the completion of the NFL draft as agreed by owners.

32.The Rookie draft will last 5 rounds with 5 minutes for each pick. The timer may be paused to allow for trading.

Rookie Draft Order

33.The top 4 draft picks will be assigned to the 4 non-playoff teams in the following manner:

34.The 4 non-playoff teams will compete in a head-to-head playoff for the 1.01 draft pick. The remaining picks 1.2-1.4 will be determined by the maximum potential regular season points (as determined by Sleeper) in inverse order.

35.During week 15, intra-conference matchups will take place (subject to ‘Rule 3’ below). During Week 16 the two winners of Week 15 will face off in an inter-conference matchup to be crowned Champion, the two losing teams will face off to decide the Golden Turd winner. With this system, the 4th worst team still has a shot at the 1.1 as does every other non-play-off team (avoiding the benefit of tanking).

36.The previous season champion and runner up will receive the 1.10 and 1.09 pick, respectively. The two losing semi-finalists will be allocated 1.07-1.08 by the maximum potential regular season points (as determined by Sleeper) in inverse order. The two losing quarterfinalists will be allocated 1.05-1.06 by the maximum potential regular season points (as determined by Sleeper) in inverse order.

37.The Rookie draft is a straight draft, going from picks 1-10, and then back to 1 again. It is NOT a serpentine draft. The team with pick 1.01 will have pick 2.01, 3.01, etc. unless any of those picks have been traded.

Rookie Draft Eligibility

38.The Rookie draft shall include all rookies selected during the NFL draft and rookie undrafted free agents. Any players drafted in the supplemental NFL draft after the start of the Rookie draft will be entered into the JADL free agent pool and be eligible for pickup at the Free Agent auction or start of the blind bid process.

39.JADL teams will be allowed to carry all drafted rookies either on their Taxi or extended bench during the off-season.

Taxi

40.A 7 space ‘Taxi’ will be available from the inaugural Rookie draft to allow Rookies acquired either by Rookie draft, Free Agency auction or waivers up to the start of the first game of the regular season. No rookies may be placed onto the taxi after that point.

41.Any rookie removed from the Taxi whether it be into a starting lineup, bench spot, IR or dropped to free agency may not be returned to the Taxi after the deadline.

42.Rookies may remain on the Taxi for a maximum of two years. After this period they must be either placed on the active roster or dropped to free agency. Only rookies who remain on the Taxi on the day of the rookie draft may be carried over for a second year.

43.The 7 spots may be used at the discretion of a GM. It may contain 7 sophomores (subject to para 40) or 7 rookies or any combination in between.

Yearly Free Agency Auction

44.In each subsequent year after the Maiden draft, an off-season live auction for free agents called the Free Agency auction will be held. This will be two weeks prior to the start of the regular season as agreed by owners.

45.The Free Agency auction may be conducted on the Sleeper app, via video call or in-person.

46.The order of players to be auctioned will be based on the inverse of the Rookie draft. The auction will last a maximum of 5 rounds. An owner may skip their nomination but will then sacrifice the opportunity to nominate any further in that auction. The auction will end when all players have skipped their turn or nominated their maximum 5 players.

47.The currency for the auction will be each owners’ remaining FAAB budget for the season. There will be no other currency allowed. The minimum bid for a player at the auction will be $1. It is the responsibility of each owner to ensure they do not bid more FAAB than they have in their budget at the commencement of the auction. At the end of each player auction a running total of remaining FAAB will be kept by the Commissioner. If an owner exceeds their FAAB budget, the auction for that player will be re-run with the offending owner excluded from the remainder of the entire Free Agency auction.

48.After the auction, the Commissioner the requisite FAAB will be removed from their remaining FAAB budget. If there is insufficient space on any of these an owner will be asked to drop an existing asset. This is to prevent players from obtaining players to artificially block another owner from acquiring an asset at the auction only to place them immediately back in free agency.

Free Agency Auction Eligibility

49.The Free Agency auction shall include any NFL free agents that are not on a current JADL roster, including any rookies selected during the NFL draft and rookie undrafted free agents not acquired at the Rookie draft.

50.JADL teams will be allowed to carry all drafted players during the Off-season until the Final cut-down date.

Scoring, Lineup Submission and Position Designation

51.The starting lineups will consist of: 1 QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, 1 TE, 2 Flex (RB, WR or TE), 1 Superflex Players (QB, RB, WR or TE), 1 K and 1 Defense/Special Team. Starting lineups may be submitted to the Sleeper system. The deadline for lineup submission for each player via website or app is five minutes prior to the scheduled start time of the game in which the player is participating.

52.Player position eligibility and designation is determined by Sleeper during the pre-season and may be changed any time before the start of the NFL season. JADL participants agree to accept these designations and understand the final position designations may be different from the time their drafts were conducted.

53.The JADL will use the Half Point Per reception (PPR) scoring system.

Free Agency Acquisitions and Blind Bidding

54.The following free agent blind bidding process is the only means by which free agents may be acquired in the JADL Season. There are no other ways to acquire free agents like ‘worst-to-first’ and ‘first-come-first-served’ or any other waiver methods other than blind bidding as described below.

55.Any active NFL player which is not on a roster in the individual league is considered a free agent for that league and may be available for pick-up. Players dropped by a team are placed into the free agent player pool and will be available for pick-up (see below for exclusions).

56.The free agent acquisition process is made by way of “blind bidding”. Each team is given 1,000 free agent “dollars” at the start of a season (not real money), which they may use to bid on free agents during a weekly blind bidding process. Bids will remain hidden from the rest of the league until after all the winning bids have been awarded. Free agent players will be awarded to the team with the highest bid for that free agent, and the winning bid amount will be deducted from that team’s free agent dollars. Besides the winning bid, all other bids for that free agent are considered losing bids and are cancelled. At this point, the highest remaining bid by any team for any remaining free agent will be considered. The team with this winning bid will be awarded the free agent and all losing bids for that free agent will be cancelled. This process will continue until every remaining bid has been considered.

57.All JADL teams will be allowed to bid on players during all waiver wires during the Season.

58.Due to the weekly Thursday games in the NFL schedule, there are three (3) weekly blind bidding processes. The first “Primary” blind bidding process will take place on Wednesday, the second “Supplementary” process taking place on Friday and a third ‘Tertiary’ process on a Sunday.

59.The deadline for bid submission for all waivers is determined by the Sleeper system.

60.All free agent NFL players will be available for the Wednesday bidding process.

61.NFL players which are dropped during Friday’s bidding process will NOT be available for pickups on Sunday (48 hours later) and will ONLY become available for next week’s Wednesday bidding process.

62.Free agent NFL players whose teams played on Thursday will NOT be available for pickups on Friday or Sunday (following Thursday’s game) and will ONLY become available for next week’s Wednesday bidding process.

63.After the completion of Week 1, the blind bidding processes will be available weekly and will continue until prior to Week 17 for ALL teams in the JADLs. Rosters will be updated with the results of all successful bids following the completion of the blind bidding process.

64.All players cut during Weeks 1 through Week 17 free agent bidding processes WILL REMAIN in the free agent pool and will be available to be re-acquired by teams as long as the free agency bidding processes remain open.

65.The Commissioner reserves the right to remove a dropped player(s) from the free agent pool or reinstate that player(s) back to its original team, if the Commissioner determines that doing so is the interest of upholding the integrity of the contest, or that the dropped player(s) would unfairly impact the outcome of the league. In this extremely rare instance, all participants will be notified and an explanation will be given.

Bidding Tiebreakers

66.In the event that there is a tie between bids, the team which is awarded the player will be determined in order by inverse order of the league standings.

67.Teams may submit as many bids as they like. There are no limitations as to the number of players that may be acquired by any one team during the course of the weekly blind bidding processes or during the course of the year, provided teams remain within the maximum number of 26 players allowed on each roster.

68.The minimum bid amount is $0.

Playoff Structure

69.The 14-week regular season will run from NFL Week 1 through NFL Week 14. Over the course of the regular season, each of the 10 teams in the JADL will play Head-to-Head matchups vs. the rest of the league. Each team will play five teams twice to make up the 14 games. Four of these five games will be conference matchups with the 5th game being against the team from the opposing conference with the same final finishing position of the prior season. All H2H head matchups will be scored based on a half point PPR format and the conference standings will be a result of the number of victories that each team accumulates.

70.The weekly conference standings will be based on the number of total victories each team has accumulated to date. If there is a tie between teams in weekly points scored the following tiebreakers will be used to determine the league table as follows:

1.Most total points to date

2.Head to head record vs team(s) tied with

3.Most wins to date

4.Least points against to date.

71.At the conclusion of Week 14, six teams from the JADL will earn a berth in the Playoffs in Weeks 15, 16 and 17. These six teams are determined as follows:

(1) Each Conference winner receives an automatic bye to the second week of the playoffs;

(2) The second-place team of each Conference automatically gets a playoff spot; and,

(3) The remaining third place teams secure the remaining playoff spots unless a third-place team in one division has a record less than .500 and the fourth-place team in the other division has a record greater than .500. This is known as ‘Rule 3’.

72.Subject to the ‘Rule 3’ above, second and third in each division will play each other in week 15 with the winners playing the conference winners in week 16. The two conferences will playoff in week 17 to be crowned champion.

73.If three teams from one conference enter the playoffs, second will play third, with the fourth-place team playing the second-place team from the other conference.

74.The four non-playoff teams will face each other in the consolation bracket as normal competing for the 1.01. Week 15 will be the intra-conference round with the inter-conference playoff in week 16.

75.Where ‘Rule 3’ applies, fourth and fifth of a conference with three teams will playoff in week 15, with third facing fifth from the opposing conference.

Prize Distribution

76.Winners will be notified by WhatsApp within 10 days after the final league and overall results are posted on the Sleeper website. All award money will be paid in no later than 10 days after the end of the overall season.

Sanctions

77.If any of the above rules are breached, at the Commissioners discretion, the following sanctions may apply:

i.1st offence – 2nd round pick at the next draft. The players 2nd round pick. If they do not have their own pick then the highest pick they have in that round. If they do not have a 2nd round pick then a 1st and so on. If they have no picks it will be a 1st round pick in future years.

ii.2nd or more offences - 1st round pick at the next draft. The players 1st round pick. If they do not have their own pick then the highest pick they have in that round. If they do not have a 1st round pick then it will be a 1st round pick in future years.

iii.Offences will expire after two complete seasons.

Special COVID-19 Rules

COVID Injury Reserve Transactions

78.During the Season under COVID restrictions, a team may place a player on IR if they appear on their NFL teams COVID Reserve List. The team may then replace the COVID IR player and pick up a player via the blind bid waiver wire process. COVID IR moves are free of charge. Any free agent who is listed on COVID IR by an NFL team and is then drafted or acquired via the waiver wire is eligible for the 3 additional COVID IR list spots. A team may have a maximum of 3 players on the IR list at any one time. The official site for determining a player’s COVID IR status will be NFL.com. All players on the IR list are eligible to be traded to another team. If an NFL team activates a player on COVID IR to their active roster, owners must activate or cut that player within the next 3 days during the JADL season. All players must be removed from injured reserve prior to the March 31st roster cut-down date.

79.It is each owners responsibility to ensure they have a maximum of 3 players on the COVID IR list. The Commissioner may intervene if any IR is in contravention of the additional IR rules.

IR Qualification

80.Any player on NFL COVID List, NFL Injured Reserve, NFL Injured Reserve-Eligible to Return, NFL-PUP list, NFL-NFI (non-football injury) list can be placed on Injured Reserve. A player that is Suspended or Out for the week cannot be placed on Injured Reserve.

Yearly Rookie Draft

81.In each subsequent year after the Maiden draft, an off-season draft for rookies called the Rookie draft will be held. This will be after the second Friday after of the completion of the NFL draft as agreed by owners.

82.The Rookie draft will last 5 rounds.

Rookie Draft Order

83.In the event that a season is abandoned early due to COVID-19 restrictions the Rookie draft order will be determined by the following process:

84.If the season contained 8 or more complete matchups the rookie draft order will be in the reverse order of the final standings after the last complete matchup.

85.If the season contained fewer than 8 complete matchups the season will be abandoned. The Rookie draft order will be in the reverse order of the previous draft.

86.However, the Rookie draft following an abandoned season will not be a straight draft. It will be a serpentine draft.

Rookie Draft Eligibility

87.The Rookie draft shall include all rookies selected during the NFL draft and rookie undrafted free agents. Any players drafted in the supplemental NFL draft after the start of the Rookie draft will be entered into the JADL free agent pool and be eligible for pickup at the Free Agency auction or start of the blind bid process.

88.JADL teams will be allowed to carry all drafted players during the Off-season until the Final cut-down date. The Final cut down date will one week prior to the day of the first game of the NFL season.

Prize Distribution

89.In the event that a season is disrupted by COVID-19 prizes will be distributed in the following way:

90.In the event there are 8 or more complete match ups prizes will be distributed as follows:

91.In the event there are fewer than 8 complete match-ups, no prizes will be awarded and additional subs will not be due for the following season.

92.Winners will be notified by WhatsApp within 10 days after the final league and overall results are posted on the Sleeper website. All award money will be paid in no later than 10 days after the end of the overall season.
